    Viña Bujanda

    Viña Bujanda is a wine producer in Oyón, in the Rioja Alavesa wine area of Rioja (Rioja Alta, Rioja Alavesa and Rioja Oriental). The estate farms about 120 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are red, led by Tempranillo.
